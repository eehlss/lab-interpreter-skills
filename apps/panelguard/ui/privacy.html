1. Introduction

PanelGuard is a blood bank antibody-identification copilot developed by Dr. Emeka Patrick Chukwuka, operating under ALAFIAAI/EEHLSS. PanelGuard is a research and decision-support tool only — it is not a medical device and is not intended for clinical diagnostic use.

We operate in Nigeria and Saudi Arabia and are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

2. Important Notice: Synthetic Data Only

PanelGuard demo environments use synthetic data only. Users must not enter real patient data, protected health information (PHI), or any personally identifiable information (PII) into the system. All acceptable inputs must use:

  • Synthetic patient identifiers (e.g., SYN-12345)
  • Year 2099 or other clearly synthetic dates
  • No real patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, or other identifiers

PanelGuard includes automated PHI detection to block real patient data, but users remain responsible for ensuring compliance.

3. Data Collection and Use

3.1 What We Collect

PanelGuard currently collects minimal data:

  • Technical Logs: IP addresses, timestamps, request counts for rate limiting and error monitoring
  • Synthetic Input Data: Case inputs submitted for analysis (synthetic only)
  • No User Accounts: We do not currently collect names, email addresses, or create user accounts
  • No Analytics: We do not currently use third-party analytics or tracking tools

3.2 AI Processing via Third-Party LLM

PanelGuard uses AI models provided by Nebius Token Factory (NVIDIA Nemotron models) to generate explanations and reasoning for synthetic case inputs. Synthetic case data is transmitted to Nebius servers for processing. We do not share real patient data with any third party.

For Nebius data handling practices, see: https://nebius.com/privacy

3.3 Data Retention

Technical logs are retained for [RETENTION PERIOD] for operational and debugging purposes. Synthetic case inputs are not persistently stored beyond processing time.

4. Your Rights

4.1 Under Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)

If you are in Nigeria, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)

4.2 Under Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)

If you are in Saudi Arabia, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (under certain conditions)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA)

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, secure headers (HSTS, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options), and automated PHI detection. However, no system is completely secure, and PanelGuard is a research tool not validated for clinical use.
